# Task 2 - Data Visualization and Storytelling
Data Analyst Internship - Elevate Labs

## Dataset
Superstore Sales Data (2021-2024)

## Story in One Line
Sales grew +79% from $285K (2021) to $512K (2024), led by Technology and West region.

## Key Insights
1. Year Growth: Sales every year increased, +79% in 4 years.
2. Category: Technology $660K is No.1, 42% of total sales.
3. Region: West $512K is No.1, South $248K needs improvement.
4. Seasonality: Nov-Dec is peak season - Q4 focus is important.
5. Profit: Profit grew from $32K to $76K - faster than sales.
6. Top Product: Phones $285K is top sub-category.

## Recommendations
- Invest more in Technology / Phones
- Improve South region marketing
- Plan big offers in Q4 (Nov-Dec)
- Push high-profit categories
